# Movie Night: quality review and missing features

Reviewed 19 Oct 2026, at commit `0023250`. Fixes from this review are in `deeae99`, plus four more found while testing on the Mac and iPad.

## Bugs fixed in this pass

| Severity | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | If a drive was unplugged after launch, the next rescan found an "empty" folder and **deleted every video and its watch history** from the library. The app's main promise to external SSD users broke. | Check the folder is reachable before scanning, and make the scanner throw for missing folders instead of returning nothing. Re-check availability when drives mount or unmount on Mac, and when the app comes to the front. Covered by a new test. |
| High | macOS and iOS purge `Caches` when storage is low, so thumbnails could vanish and never come back. | Missing artwork is regenerated on the next scan. |
| Medium | An iCloud file that failed to download (offline, quota) showed "Loading" forever. | The download error is shown. |
| Medium | Removing a folder while its videos were still being read could write to deleted records (crash risk). | Skip deleted items. |
| Low (performance) | Every card decoded its thumbnail at full 1280px (~3.5 MB each in memory). | Cards decode at card size; only the hero banner uses full size. |
| Low (performance) | The MKV player pushed a UI update every frame (30-60 a second). | About 4 a second. |
| High | Picture in Picture failed on iPad and sound stopped on leaving the app, even for MP4/MOV, because the audio background mode was missing from the Info.plist. | Added `UIBackgroundModes: audio`. |
| Medium | Closing the player while an iCloud file was still downloading could start playback afterwards, with sound and no visible player. | Playback checks for cancellation before starting. |
| Medium | Rewatching a finished video and stopping halfway left it "Watched", so there was no Resume and it stayed out of Continue Watching. | Progress past 5 seconds clears the watched flag. |
| Low | "More Info" on a video's own page pushed the same page again. | Hidden on the detail page. |

## Known weak spots, not fixed

1. ~~Moving or renaming a file loses its watch history.~~ Fixed after the review: watch state is also kept by file name and size, so a moved file, or a folder removed and added back, picks up where it was. A renamed file starts fresh, which Khush is fine with.
2. **No sync between Mac and iPad.** Each device has its own library and watch state. iCloud sync through SwiftData's CloudKit option is blocked by the `@Attribute(.unique)` constraints the models use today, so this needs a model change before it can be turned on.
3. **Two players behave differently.** MP4/MOV get Apple's player, with PiP, AirPlay and lock-screen controls (on iPad, PiP only works since the background-audio fix above). MKV and other formats get the mpv player, which has none of those. On iPad, an MKV stops when you leave the app.
4. **No external subtitles for MP4/MOV.** mpv picks up `.srt` files next to a video, but Apple's player doesn't. A `Movie.mp4` with `Movie.srt` shows no subtitles.
5. **HDR in MKV files is tone-mapped to SDR.** mpv's HDR passthrough isn't enabled yet.
6. **iCloud files that aren't downloaded get no artwork, length or date** until they're played once.
7. **The Mac release needs a Lua-free mpv build** before notarizing or submitting to the App Store, as covered in ARCHITECTURE.md.
8. **iPad uses the iPhone sizes.** Cards are 220pt and the hero is 360pt, which feels small on an 11-13" iPad.
9. **Accessibility:** the hero title is a fixed 40pt and won't scale with Dynamic Type, and quality chips aren't read out by VoiceOver.

## Missing features, in suggested order

### Tier 1: expected by anyone coming from Netflix or Apple TV

- **Search** across titles, folders and dates (⌘F on Mac).
- **Play next / autoplay**: when a clip ends, roll into the next one in the same folder or day, with a short countdown. This matters for home video, where clips are 20 seconds long.
- **Scrubbing previews**: thumbnails while you drag the timeline.
- **Unified controls** for both players, so MKV and MP4 feel the same. This also brings PiP and Now Playing/lock-screen controls to MKV.
- **Live folder watching**: new files appear without pressing Rescan.

### Tier 2: what makes this better than Infuse for home video

- **Events**: group clips shot within a few hours into one "event" card, such as "Saturday 14 July 2019, 23 clips, 18m". Most home video is dozens of short clips, and browsing them one by one is the main pain today.
- **Smarter artwork**: sample several frames and pick the sharpest, best-lit one, using Vision for faces and saliency. The fixed 10% frame is often blurry.
- **Places**: phone video carries GPS. Show "Lisbon, 2019" on cards and add a map or "Places" row.
- **People**: Vision face clustering into "Clips with Grandma" rows. Ask before turning it on, since it's sensitive.
- **Editing the library**: rename a clip, merge folders into a collection, hide clips, favourites.
- **iCloud Photos as a source** (PhotoKit), since that's where most phone video actually lives.

### Tier 3: later

- Sync watch state across devices (needs the model change in weak spot 2).
- An Apple TV app, with the Mac serving the library over the local network.
- Chapters for long videos, and a "skip intro" marker for owned films.
- Parental lock or a hidden folder for private videos.
- AirPlay for MKV, which would mean transcoding on the fly. It's expensive, so only if people ask for it.

## My recommendation

Do **Events** and **Play next** next. Together they turn a folder of 400 short clips into something you can actually sit and watch, which is the reason to use this over Infuse or VLC. Search and unified controls come right after.
